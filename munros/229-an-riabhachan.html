Munro Loch Ness

An Riabhachan

1128m / 3700ft Grid ref NH133344

Location

Did You Know

An Riabhachan, 'the brindled one', is a broad and somewhat featureless summit that nevertheless provides a commanding viewpoint over the remote northern Highlands. The raising of Loch Mullardoch's level in the 1950s significantly changed the approach routes to these mountains.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed as part of the Loch Mullardoch circuit, linked to Sgùrr na Lapaich and An Socach.

Character of the route

A massive ridged mountain with a grassy crest broken by three distinct tops, the central one the highest.

Practical notes

The traverse is most logically done using the Loch Mullardoch ferry to reach An Socach before crossing the ridge eastwards back to the dam.
