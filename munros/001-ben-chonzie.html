Munro Perthshire

Ben Chonzie

930m / 3052ft Grid ref NN773308

Location

Did You Know

Ben Chonzie is one of the most rounded and featureless Munros in Scotland, earning an affectionate reputation as one of the 'dull' Munros. It is nevertheless popular as a straightforward winter introduction to Munro bagging.

Field Notes

The approach

The most popular route climbs from Glen Turret, starting at the dam car park and following a broad, easy track along the east side of Loch Turret for around six kilometres before a steeper pull to the summit plateau.

Character of the route

Ben Chonzie has a reputation as one of the less dramatic Munros, a broad, grassy, heathery hill rather than a rugged peak, though the flat summit does open out to good views towards Schiehallion and the Grampians.

Practical notes

A common descent follows a ruined fence along a wide grassy ridge to Carn Chois before dropping back to the dam. The gentle slopes and easy navigation in clear weather make this a popular winter outing.
