Munro Fort William

Sgùrr na Cìche

1040m / 3413ft Grid ref NM902966
Location
Did You Know

Sgurr na Ciche, 'peak of the breast', is one of the finest and most dramatic mountains in Knoydart, a region accessible only on foot or by boat. Its shapely conical summit is one of the most instantly recognisable profiles in the western Highlands.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed as part of a longer circuit from the head of Glen Dessarry, taking in Garbh Chioch Mhòr and Sgùrr nan Coireachan on the same round.

Character of the route

The highest and most striking peak in the Rough Bounds of Knoydart, a distinctive cone shaped summit with sweeping views out to Eigg, Rum and Skye.

Practical notes

A remote and serious undertaking given the distance from any road, usually requiring a very long day or an overnight stay to do the group justice.
