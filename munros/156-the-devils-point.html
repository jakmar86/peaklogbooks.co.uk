Munro Cairngorms

The Devil's Point

1007m / 3303ft Grid ref NN976951

Location

Did You Know

The Devil's Point earned its colourful English name as a Victorian translation of its Gaelic name, Bod an Deamhain, 'the devil's penis' — deemed too crude for the sensibilities of 19th century visitors. It sits above the head of the Lairig Ghru in spectacular position.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually reached from Linn of Dee via Glen Luibeg or Glen Dee to Corrour Bothy, then a path up Coire Odhar.

Character of the route

A bare pyramid of rock guarding the southern entrance to the Lairig Ghru, its Gaelic name Bod an Deamhain rather more blunt than its English translation.

Practical notes

Corrour Bothy sits at its foot in the middle of the pass, a useful landmark though not accessible by road, with Linn of Dee the nearest car park on foot or by bike.
