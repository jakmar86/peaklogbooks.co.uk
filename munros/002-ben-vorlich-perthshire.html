Munro Perthshire

Ben Vorlich (Loch Earn)

985m / 3233ft Grid ref NN629189

Location

Did You Know

This Ben Vorlich above Loch Earn is one of the most popular Munros in Scotland and should not be confused with its namesake above Loch Lomond. On a clear day the view stretches from Ben Nevis in the north to the Pentland Hills near Edinburgh.

Field Notes

The approach

Most walkers start from the minor road on the south side of Loch Earn, following a track beside the Ardvorlich Burn before climbing onto the broad flank of the hill.

Character of the route

Ben Vorlich itself is a fairly straightforward ascent on a well maintained path, in clear contrast to its rockier neighbour, Stuc a' Chroin.

Practical notes

The two hills are very often combined in a single long day of around 16km and over 1,200m of ascent, so allow a full day if you plan to do both.
