Munro Fort William

Meall Dearg (Aonach Eagach)

952m / 3124ft Grid ref NN161583
Location
Did You Know

Meall Dearg, 'red hill', is the eastern Munro of the Aonach Eagach and is reached by a steep ascent directly from the glen. Once on the ridge the route becomes a continuous scramble with no easy escape until the western end of the ridge is reached.

Field Notes

The approach

The central and eastern summit of the Aonach Eagach ridge, usually reached as part of the full traverse from Sgorr nam Fiannaidh.

Character of the route

Holds a notable place in Munro history: it was the final summit climbed by the Reverend A.E. Robertson in 1901, the first person recorded to complete all the Munros.

Practical notes

The narrow, rocky ridge between the two summits demands full concentration and a head for exposure throughout.
