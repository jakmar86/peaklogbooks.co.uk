Munro Loch Lomond

Ben Vorlich (Loch Lomond)

943m / 3093ft Grid ref NN295124

Location

Did You Know

There are two Munros named Ben Vorlich in Scotland — this one above Loch Lomond and another above Loch Earn in Perthshire. Every Munroist must climb both, making it one of the most commonly confused entries on the list.

Field Notes

The approach

Commonly climbed from Inveruglas, following the main route up the hill before continuing across a dam towards Ben Vane.

Character of the route

Not to be confused with its namesake above Loch Earn, this Ben Vorlich sits within the Arrochar Alps above Loch Lomond.

Practical notes

Often paired with Ben Vane in a single outing given their shared start point.
