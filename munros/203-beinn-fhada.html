Munro Kintail

Beinn Fhada

1032m / 3385ft Grid ref NH018192
Location
Did You Know

Beinn Fhada, 'long mountain', is exactly as described — a long sprawling massif above Strath Croe near Morvich. Its vast Plaide Mhor plateau and the dramatic Gleann Choinneachain approach make it one of the most satisfying Munros in Kintail.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually combined with A' Ghlas-bheinn in a circuit around Gleann Lichd from Morvich.

Character of the route

Known also as Attow, a long, broad ridge overlooking the glen, quite different in character to the narrower peaks of the Five Sisters nearby.

Practical notes

The circuit with A' Ghlas-bheinn makes for a satisfying full day out from Morvich.
