Munro Ullapool

Eididh nan Clach Geala

927m / 3041ft Grid ref NH257842

Location

Did You Know

Eididh nan Clach Geala, 'web of the white stones', is one of the more remote of the Beinn Dearg group and requires a lengthy approach walk. Its unusual name refers to the network of pale quartzite boulders that covers much of its summit plateau.

Field Notes

The approach

The fourth summit of the Beinn Dearg group, usually the final peak on the full circuit.

Character of the route

Its name refers to the white stones scattered across its slopes, a distinctive feature of the mountain.

Practical notes

Usually climbed as part of the wider four Munro round rather than in isolation.
