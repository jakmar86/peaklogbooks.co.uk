Munro Angus

Mayar

929m / 3047ft Grid ref NO240737

Location

Did You Know

Mayar forms a natural pairing with Driesh above Glen Clova. The two mountains are almost always climbed together and provide a fine circuit above one of the most attractive glens in Angus. Glen Clova was described by Sir Walter Scott as one of the most beautiful glens in Scotland.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed from Glen Doll alongside its neighbour Driesh, with clear tracks and well defined paths making it one of the more straightforward introductions to Munro walking.

Character of the route

A broad, grassy summit reached via the dramatic natural amphitheatre of Corrie Fee, a National Nature Reserve on the approach.

Practical notes

No scrambling or technical ground involved, a good option for building fitness or for those new to Munro bagging. Parking at Glen Doll carries a small charge, with a visitor centre and toilets on site.
