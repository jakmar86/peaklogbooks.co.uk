Cairn of Claise

1063m / 3488ft Grid ref NO185788
Location
Did You Know

Cairn of Claise, 'cairn of the hollow', sits on the White Mounth plateau above Jock's Road, a historic drove road through the mountains. Jock's Road was the subject of a landmark legal case in 1887 that established the right of public access across private land in Scotland.

Field Notes

The approach

Climbed as part of the same round from the Cairnwell Pass, usually linking Glas Maol and Carn an Tuirc.

Character of the route

One of several broad, rounded summits on this stretch of the Mounth plateau.

Practical notes

Straightforward walking, best combined with its neighbours to make a fuller day given how close together the summits lie.
