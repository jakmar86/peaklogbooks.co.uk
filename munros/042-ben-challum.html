Munro Argyll

Ben Challum

1025m / 3363ft Grid ref NN386322
Location
Did You Know

Ben Challum is named after Saint Columba, the Irish monk who brought Christianity to Scotland in the 6th century. The mountain overlooks Strath Fillan, historically an important route through the Highlands and part of the ancient pilgrimage road to St Andrews.

Field Notes

The approach

An out-and-back route from a layby on the A82 near Kirkton Farm, starting with a flat walk across farmland before the south ridge climbs directly to the summit.

Character of the route

A false summit partway up can be misleading, with a further 200m of ascent still to come beyond it.

Practical notes

The lower slopes are wet for much of the year, so waterproof boots are worth having regardless of season.
