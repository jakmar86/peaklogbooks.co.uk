Munro Fort William

Càrn Liath (Creag Meagaidh)

1006m / 3301ft Grid ref NN472903

Location

Did You Know

Carn Liath, 'grey cairn', forms the eastern end of the classic Creag Meagaidh horseshoe. The dramatic Coire Ardair below this ridge is one of the finest winter climbing venues in Scotland, with routes of all grades on its impressive crags.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed as part of the Creag Meagaidh round from Aberarder.

Character of the route

One of the plateau summits northeast of Creag Meagaidh's main top, sharing the same wild, high character as its neighbours.

Practical notes

Typically the first or last summit of the day depending on the direction of travel around the circuit.
