Munro Loch Ness

Sgùrr nan Ceathreamhnan

1150m / 3772ft Grid ref NH057228
Location
Did You Know

Sgurr nan Ceathreamhnan, 'peak of the quarters', is one of the most remote and challenging Munros in Scotland, requiring a long walk-in from any direction. It is nevertheless considered one of the finest mountains in the country by those who make the effort.

Field Notes

The approach

A remote peak in northwest Glen Affric, usually approached from Alltbeithe, reached via a long walk in from the Cluanie Inn, Morvich or up Glen Affric itself.

Character of the route

A magnificent, complex mountain considered one of the finest in the northern Highlands, set among some of the remotest ground in Britain.

Practical notes

Alltbeithe is the remotest hostel in Britain and a useful base for tackling this and its neighbours over more than one day.
