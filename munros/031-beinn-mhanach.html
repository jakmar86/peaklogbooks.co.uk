Munro Argyll

Beinn Mhanach

952m / 3125ft Grid ref NN373411
Location
Did You Know

Beinn Mhanach translates as 'monk's mountain', referencing a religious community that once occupied the valley below. It is one of the most remote Munros in the Southern Highlands, requiring a long approach walk through Glen Lyon.

Field Notes

The approach

Sits on the north side of Loch Lyon, around five miles east of Bridge of Orchy.

Character of the route

One of the quieter Munros in this cluster, somewhat set apart from the main Bridge of Orchy chain.

Practical notes

Sometimes combined with the Bridge of Orchy group for a longer traverse, given its proximity.
