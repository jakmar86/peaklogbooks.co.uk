Munro Cairngorms

Geal Chàrn (Monadhliath)

926m / 3039ft Grid ref NN561987
Location
Did You Know

This Geal Charn is the most northerly of the four Munros sharing this name and sits on the empty Monadhliath plateau. The plateau is one of the largest continuous areas of blanket bog in Britain, an internationally important habitat for wildlife.

Field Notes

The approach

The most detached of the four Monadhliath Munros, usually requiring a longer approach than its neighbours.

Character of the route

Set in some of the least visited ground in Britain, sharing the vast, empty plateau character of the wider range.

Practical notes

A full round of all four Monadhliath Munros makes for a genuinely long day, so plan accordingly if attempting them together.
