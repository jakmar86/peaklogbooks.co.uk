Munro Fort William

Sgùrr nan Coireachan (Glen Dessary)

954m / 3129ft Grid ref NM933958

Location

Did You Know

This remote Sgurr nan Coireachan above Glen Dessary requires a long approach on foot from the nearest road. The Knoydart peninsula it overlooks is known as the last wilderness in Britain and has no road access whatsoever.

Field Notes

The approach

The third summit of the Glen Dessarry Munros, usually climbed alongside Sgùrr na Cìche and Garbh Chioch Mhòr.

Character of the route

Shares the rugged, remote character of its neighbours in the Rough Bounds of Knoydart.

Practical notes

Best tackled as part of the full three Munro round given how far this area lies from any road.
