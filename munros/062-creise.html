Munro Fort William

Creise

1100m / 3608ft Grid ref NN238506
Location
Did You Know

Creise forms a fine horseshoe with Meall a' Bhuiridh above Glen Etive and together they make one of the best mountain days in the Glencoe area. The dramatic cliffs of Creise are visible from the main road through Glen Coe below.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed alongside Meall a' Bhuiridh, reached via the ridge from Clach Leathad.

Character of the route

Overlooks the northern end of Glen Etive and forms part of the Black Mount group of four Munros.

Practical notes

Often the third summit tackled on the classic Black Mount traverse, between Stob Ghabhar and Meall a' Bhuiridh.
