Munro Kintail

Creag a' Mhàim

946m / 3104ft Grid ref NH087077
Location
Did You Know

Creag a' Mhaim, 'rock of the large rounded hill', is the westernmost of the South Glen Shiel Ridge and provides a fine viewpoint across Loch Duich to the Five Sisters of Kintail. It is often used as the starting point for the full ridge traverse.

Field Notes

The approach

The easternmost Munro of the South Glen Shiel Ridge, usually climbed from a layby near the Cluanie Inn, the traditional start or finish point for the full seven Munro traverse.

Character of the route

An easy, gradual ascent on an old estate road for the first stretch, in contrast to some of the rockier ridge summits further west, with fine views over the headwaters of Loch Cluanie and Loch Loyne.

Practical notes

Often the first summit tackled on the classic west to east version of the ridge walk, or the last if walking in the other direction, finishing conveniently at the Cluanie Inn.
