Munro Kintail

Sgùrr na Sgìne

946m / 3103ft Grid ref NG946113

Location

Did You Know

Sgurr na Sgine, 'peak of the knife', forms a natural pairing with The Saddle above Glen Shiel. Its sharp profile when seen from the glen below justifies the name, presenting a fine rocky peak above the broad lower slopes.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed alongside The Saddle via the Forcan Ridge, reached by a short detour from the northwest top.

Character of the route

A rocky summit perched above a steep face, with the ridge continuing beyond it to the minor top of Faochag.

Practical notes

Normally taken as the second Munro of a day that begins with The Saddle, rather than climbed on its own.
