Sgùrr Dubh Mòr

944m / 3097ft Grid ref NG457205

Location

Did You Know

Sgurr Dubh Mor, 'big black peak', is one of the more remote and serious summits on the Cuillin Main Ridge. The approach via Loch Coruisk, accessible only by boat or on foot, is one of the most dramatic in Scotland.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually reached from Glen Brittle via Coire Ghrunnda as part of a longer traverse.

Character of the route

Set slightly back from the main ridge line, with a Munro Top lying a short distance to its west.

Practical notes

A remote and serious objective even by Cuillin standards, usually tackled only by those already comfortable on this ground.
