Munro Loch Ness

Sgùrr a' Choire Ghlais

1084m / 3555ft Grid ref NH258430

Location

Did You Know

Sgurr a' Choire Ghlais, 'peak of the green corrie', forms part of the attractive Strathfarrar ridge where access is controlled to protect the environment. The glen is one of the least visited major glens in the Highlands as a result.

Field Notes

The approach

The highest of the Strathfarrar Munros, usually reached via the long private road up Glen Strathfarrar from Struy.

Character of the route

A grand peak at the centre of the range, its summit topped with two fine cairns.

Practical notes

One of the more difficult Munros to reach in Scotland, with a ten mile approach up the glen. A bicycle, or permission to drive the private road, can shorten the walk considerably.
