Munro Loch Lomond

Ben Vane

916m / 3004ft Grid ref NN277098

Location

Did You Know

Ben Vane sits just one metre above the qualifying Munro height, making it one of the lowest on the list. Despite its modest elevation, its steep and unrelenting flanks make it one of the most physically demanding climbs relative to its height.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed as part of a circuit with Beinn Narnain and Beinn Ime from Succoth, or paired with Ben Vorlich from Inveruglas.

Character of the route

Despite being the lowest of the Arrochar Alps, the climb up Ben Vane is often considered the most taxing of the group, over rough, pathless ground.

Practical notes

The return along Loin Water to Loch Long completes the popular three-Munro circuit with Beinn Narnain and Beinn Ime.
