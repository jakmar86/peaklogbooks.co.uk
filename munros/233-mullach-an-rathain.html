Munro Torridon

Mullach an Rathain (Liathach)

1024m / 3359ft Grid ref NG911576

Location

Did You Know

Mullach an Rathain, 'summit of the row of pinnacles', is the western top of Liathach. The descent of the western ridge provides one of the finest views in Scotland — the great sandstone terraces of Beinn Alligin and the Torridon sea loch below.

Field Notes

The approach

The second Munro on the Liathach ridge, usually reached by continuing the traverse from Spidean a' Choire Lèith.

Character of the route

Marks the western end of the ridge, with the pinnacled section of Am Fasarinen between the two summits one of the highlights of the route.

Practical notes

Usually descended by the path down the western nose of the mountain rather than retracing the ridge.
