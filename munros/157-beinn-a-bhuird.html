Munro Cairngorms

Beinn a' Bhùird

1196m / 3924ft Grid ref NJ092006
Location
Did You Know

Beinn a' Bhuird, 'mountain of the table', has one of the largest summit plateaux of any Scottish mountain. Its remote position in the eastern Cairngorms means it requires a very long approach walk from any direction, rewarding visitors with profound solitude.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed from Deeside via Gleann an t-Slugain, the shortest route with good paths for most of the way.

Character of the route

A vast, largely featureless summit plateau in itself, but graced with a breathtaking line of cliffs on its eastern flank, dropping thousands of feet into a huge corrie between its north and south tops.

Practical notes

Very exposed on the plateau, with difficult navigation if cloud is down, so this is not a mountain to underestimate despite the gentle paths lower down.
