Munro Torridon

Sgùrr Chòinnich

999m / 3279ft Grid ref NH076446

Location

Did You Know

Sgurr Chòinnich, mossy peak, sits in a remote position above Loch Monar in the eastern Torridon area. The long approach through Glen Carron passes through spectacular scenery of sandstone and heather moorland with impressive views of the Strathfarrar peaks.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed alongside its higher neighbour Sgùrr a' Chaorachain, from Glen Strathfarrar or the Bearnais approach.

Character of the route

Although lower than its neighbour, considered the finer peak of the pair, with crags buttressing an elegant summit ridge.

Practical notes

Nearly always climbed together with Sgùrr a' Chaorachain rather than alone.
