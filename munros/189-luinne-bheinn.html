Munro Fort William

Luinne Bheinn

939m / 3079ft Grid ref NG869007

Location

Did You Know

Luinne Bheinn, 'hill of anger or melody', sits in the heart of Knoydart above Barrisdale Bay. Access to Knoydart requires either a lengthy overland walk or a boat crossing, making every Munro bagged here feel genuinely hard-won.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed alongside Meall Buidhe from Barrisdale or Inverie, the two linked across the Bealach a' Choire Odhair.

Character of the route

One of the Knoydart three, sharing the remote, seldom visited character of this part of the Highlands.

Practical notes

Best combined with Meall Buidhe and, for those with more time, Ladhar Bheinn, over a multi day trip.
