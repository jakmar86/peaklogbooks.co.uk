Munro Cairngorms

Beinn Bhrotain

1157m / 3796ft Grid ref NN954922
Location
Did You Know

Beinn Bhrotain, 'mountain of the mastiff', is one of the remote southern Cairngorm Munros requiring a long approach from Glen Feshie or Braemar. The vast empty plateau surrounding it represents the largest area of truly wild land in Britain.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed from Linn of Dee via Glen Dee, or combined with Monadh Mòr on the same round.

Character of the route

A broad, remote hill on the southern edge of the high Cairngorm plateau, with a much quieter feel than the busier summits to the north.

Practical notes

Long approaches across open ground are typical, best planned as part of a longer day given the distances involved.
