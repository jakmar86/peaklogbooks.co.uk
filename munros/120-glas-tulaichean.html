Glas Tulaichean

1051m / 3448ft Grid ref NO051760
Location
Did You Know

Glas Tulaichean, 'grey-green hillocks', sits above Glen Shee in eastern Perthshire. The Spittal of Glenshee below was historically an important resting place for travellers crossing the high pass, and an inn has stood here since at least the 16th century.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed from Dalmunzie by way of a good estate track, or combined with Carn an Righ and Beinn Iutharn Mhor from Glen Ey.

Character of the route

One of the more straightforward Munros to reach, with a track running almost to the summit from the hotel below.

Practical notes

A good starting point for a longer round taking in its neighbouring Munros in this close knit group of hills.
