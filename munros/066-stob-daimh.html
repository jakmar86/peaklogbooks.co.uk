Munro Argyll

Stob Daimh

999m / 3278ft Grid ref NN094308
Location
Did You Know

Stob Daimh, 'peak of the stag', forms the eastern summit of the Cruachan horseshoe. The full Cruachan ridge traverse is one of the classic mountain days in Argyll, with dramatic views in all directions across the western Highlands.

Field Notes

The approach

Reached beyond Ben Cruachan along the ridge of the Cruachan Horseshoe.

Character of the route

The connecting traverse from Ben Cruachan involves a brief section of easy scrambling in places.

Practical notes

Usually combined with Ben Cruachan itself rather than climbed as a standalone summit.
