Munro Torridon

Maol Chean-dearg

933m / 3061ft Grid ref NG924499

Location

Did You Know

Maol Chean-dearg, 'bald red head', is a striking quartzite peak above Loch Torridon. Its distinctive reddish-capped summit stands in contrast to the grey and white mountains around it and can be seen from many miles away.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed from Coulags or Annat, sometimes combined with the Corbett Beinn Damh.

Character of the route

A distinctive, red topped summit standing somewhat apart from the main Torridon giants.

Practical notes

A worthwhile standalone objective, with several possible approach routes depending on the wider round chosen.
