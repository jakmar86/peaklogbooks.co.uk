Munro Cairngorms

Càrn a' Mhàim

1039m / 3409ft Grid ref NN994951
Location
Did You Know

Carn a' Mhaim forms a superb ridge connecting Cairn Toul and Ben Macdui across the Lairig Ghru. The Lairig Ghru pass it overlooks is the classic through-route of the Cairngorms, used by walkers and mountaineers for centuries.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually climbed from Linn of Dee via Glen Luibeg, often combined with the Devil's Point and Cairn Toul via Corrour Bothy.

Character of the route

A narrow ridge of a summit compared with the broader plateau hills nearby, giving good views down into the Lairig Ghru.

Practical notes

Often taken in en route to the Devil's Point, with a path skirting its flanks rather than requiring a separate ascent for those heading further into the pass.
