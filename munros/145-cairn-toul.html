Munro Cairngorms

Cairn Toul

1292m / 4240ft Grid ref NN963972

Location

Did You Know

Cairn Toul, 'barn cairn', sits in magnificent position above the Angel's Peak and the Devil's Point in the heart of the Cairngorms. The view from its summit across the Lairig Ghru to Ben Macdui is one of the finest in Scotland.

Field Notes

The approach

Usually reached from Deeside via Corrour Bothy and the Lairig Ghru, or as part of a longer traverse with Braeriach and Sgòr an Lochain Uaine.

Character of the route

A shapely, pointed summit by Cairngorm standards, its profile visible from the top of the Lairig Ghru pass alongside its neighbour the Devil's Point.

Practical notes

Often combined with the Angel's Peak given how close the two summits lie, adding relatively little extra distance to a longer round.
