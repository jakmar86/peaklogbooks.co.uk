Bidein a' Ghlas Thuill (An Teallach)

1063m / 3486ft Grid ref NH068843

Location

Did You Know

An Teallach, 'the forge', is almost universally regarded as the finest mountain in Scotland. Its dramatic pinnacled ridge, the Corrag Bhuidhe buttresses, provides one of the most exhilarating and exposed scrambles in the country. The traverse of its two Munros is a classic Scottish mountain day.

Field Notes

The approach

The highest summit of An Teallach, usually climbed from Dundonnell via Coire Chaorachain.

Character of the route

The high point of one of Scotland's most celebrated mountains, part of a long, complex ridge of pinnacles and towers.

Practical notes

The full traverse of An Teallach's ridge is a serious mountaineering expedition rather than a simple hillwalk, not to be underestimated.
